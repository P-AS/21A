# GFZ1
My original attempt for this stage from 2025 ended up being slower than Ors's IL 10.11 by one frame. Maximizing the time spent on the hyperwall helped maximize speed and saved an insane amount of time. First ever sub-10!

# GFZ2
At exactly 7.00 I have to be fully submerged underwater, which is only possible by jumping for a specific number of frames, and get a bounce off the [Stupid Dumb Unnamed RoboFish](https://wiki.srb2.org/wiki/Stupid_Dumb_Unnamed_RoboFish). When you exit water, you get a large boost in vertical speed, which I can use to fly over most of the stage. I also hit the hyperwall at pond at the top of the waterfall, and an additional boost from the current.

# GFZ3
Forcing Eggman to retreat after every hit saved 2.5 seconds over grindersky's 2023 TAS. This ties the real time WR by Ors from 2018. I did try to thok directly into Eggman instead of jumping, and while it was initially faster, doing it that way causes hits 5 and 3 to have surplus frames where Eggman can be hit, but Sonic can't properly reach him. This happened in grindersky's TAS too.
